1. Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash — never in clear text and never readable by us.
  • App activity: your virtual portfolio, trades, holdings and watchlist. These are virtual-currency records, not financial data.
  • Technical: a session cookie (a signed token) to keep you signed in, and basic server logs. We may receive anonymised crash and error reports to fix bugs (see “Processors”).

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers, and we use no advertising or profiling trackers.

2. How we use your data

Only to run the service: authenticate you, keep you signed in, store your virtual portfolio, and diagnose errors. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sports content

Player, team and match information shown in the app comes from third-party data providers (Sportmonks; pre-tournament player market values from Transfermarkt). This is public sports content, not your personal data.

4. Processors we rely on

  • Hosting: our application and database run on our own server infrastructure.
  • Error monitoring (Sentry): when enabled, technical error reports help us fix crashes. These contain diagnostic data, not your password.

5. Data retention & your rights

We keep your data while your account exists. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will delete your account and associated data on request.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt) and all traffic is served over HTTPS.

7. Children

fundXI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
